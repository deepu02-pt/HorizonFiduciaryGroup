About the group

Horizon Fiduciary Group provides fiduciary, trust, and wealth management services to its registered members, under written terms set out in full below.

  • Membership is individual — registered per person, and not transferable to anyone else.
  • Member data is kept confidential, and shared only where the law requires it.
  • Terms are set out in writing, and members are notified through official channels before any change takes effect.
  • Governed by the laws of India, with disputes resolved in Gurugram courts.

Terms & conditions

1. Introduction

+

By joining Horizon Fiduciary Group ("Company"), members agree to the following terms and conditions. These govern membership, services, benefits, and obligations.

2. Membership & registration

+
  • Registration fee: ₹100 per person (one-time, non-refundable).
  • Monthly membership plan: ₹500 per person, payable in advance.
  • Membership is non-transferable and valid only for the registered individual.

3. Services & member benefits

+
  • Members gain access to fiduciary, trust, and wealth management services.
  • Benefits become available after 1 year of continuous membership.
  • Standard withdrawal: members may withdraw up to 50% of their assets after 1 year.
  • Emergency withdrawal: members may withdraw up to 100% of their assets in case of genuine emergencies.
  • Extra withdrawal: members may withdraw more than their available assets, subject to interest charges — no interest in the first month, and a minimum 5% interest on the extra amount after 1 month.

4. Member responsibilities

+
  • Provide accurate and complete information during registration.
  • Ensure timely payment of monthly membership fees.
  • Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.

5. Confidentiality

+

Member data will remain confidential except where disclosure is required by law.

6. Fees & payments

+
  • Registration fee: ₹100 (one-time, non-refundable).
  • Monthly membership plan: ₹500, payable in advance each month.
  • Late payments may result in suspension of membership benefits.

7. Limitation of liability

+

The Company is not liable for losses due to market risks, regulatory changes, or member misrepresentation.

8. Termination

+
  • Membership may be terminated by either party with 30 days' written notice.
  • Outstanding fees and obligations must be settled before termination.

9. Governing law

+
  • These terms are governed by the laws of India.
  • Disputes will be resolved in Gurugram courts.

10. Amendments

The Company reserves the right to amend these terms at any time. Members will be notified of changes through official communication channels.
